# Game of Thrones

**Game of Thrones** is an *epic fantasy television series* based on **George R. R. Martin’s** novels, *A Song of Ice and Fire*.
It aired on **HBO** from **2011 to 2019**, spanning **8 seasons** and changing television storytelling forever.

---

## Main Setting

The story takes place in the **Seven Kingdoms of Westeros** and the continent of **Essos**.

> “When you play the game of thrones, you win or you die.”
> — *Cersei Lannister*

---

## Major Houses

### House Lannister

* Motto: *Hear Me Roar!* (official) / *A Lannister Always Pays His Debts* (common saying)
* Known for **wealth** and **influence**.

### House Stark

* Motto: *Winter is Coming*
* Known for **honor**, **loyalty**, and ruling the **North**.

### House Targaryen

* Motto: *Fire and Blood*
* Known for **dragons** and **mad kings**.

---

## My Favorite Characters
